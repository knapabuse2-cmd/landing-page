FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers.

What is Smart Money Concept (SMC)? +
SMC is a trading methodology that focuses on identifying and following institutional traders' footprints in the market. It includes concepts like Order Blocks, Fair Value Gaps, Liquidity Sweeps, and Market Structure analysis.
How do the signals get delivered? +
All signals are delivered instantly via Telegram. Each signal includes entry price, stop loss, take profit levels, and a TradingView chart with all key levels marked. You'll also get real-time updates as the trade progresses.
What's the average win rate? +
Our signals average a 72% win rate across all trading pairs. However, results vary based on market conditions and your risk management. We focus on high-probability setups with favorable risk-to-reward ratios.
Can I use this for forex or stocks? +
Currently, Shadow Protocol focuses on cryptocurrency pairs. However, the ICT/SMC concepts we detect are universal. We're working on expanding to forex and indices in the near future.
Is there a free trial? +
Yes! Our Starter plan is completely free and gives you access to 3 trading pairs with basic detection features. Pro users also get a 7-day free trial to test all premium features before committing.
